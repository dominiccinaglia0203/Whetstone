In this problem we detail first the category of affine varieties, then
 we construct the category of projective varieties. Then we detail how 
 they fit into the category of quasi projective varieties.

Key Results Proven: 
1. Affine Varieties and Spec

2. Projective Varieties and Proj

3. Connecting Affines & Projectives

4. Quasi-Projective Varieties

Bonus: Morphisms and Gluing
